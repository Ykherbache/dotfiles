# Chapter 2 — Learning to Drive

The core metaphor of XP. Beck's mother teaching him to drive: "Driving is not about getting the car going in the right direction. Driving is about constantly paying attention, making a little correction this way, a little correction that way."

## The Paradigm

**Stay aware. Adapt. Change.**

The problem isn't change — change is going to happen. The problem is our inability to cope with change.

## Two Levels of the Metaphor

1. **Customers drive content** — they keep the horizon in mind while deciding week-by-week where the software goes next
2. **The whole team drives the process** — beginning with current practices, becoming aware of which enhance and which detract from goals

## Actionable Takeaway

Each practice is an experiment in improving effectiveness. Don't aim to point the car perfectly once — aim to make frequent, small corrections toward deployed software at short intervals.
