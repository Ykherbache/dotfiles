# Chapter 24: We Feel Overwhelmed. It Isn't Going to Get Any Better

## The Green-Field Myth
Teams frustrated with legacy systems dream of rewriting from scratch. But:
- The green-field team must replicate a constantly-changing system
- Double-duty (new + old features) becomes crushing
- Often the rewrite fails, and the legacy system is the future

> The grass isn't really much greener in green-field development.

## Finding Motivation
- Connect with what's fun about programming
- Work with people you respect who know how to have fun
- Connect with the larger programming community
- TDD some code outside of work to feel the difference

## Practical Steps for Morale
> Pick the ugliest, most obnoxious set of classes in the project, and get them under test. When you've tackled the worst problem as a team, you'll feel in control of your situation.

## The Island Metaphor
Tested code areas are like **islands rising from the ocean**. Over time:
- Islands become landmasses
- Landmasses become continents
- Work in tested areas becomes dramatically easier
- The key is to keep growing the islands with every change

## Key Truth
> The attitude we bring to the work is important. Teams with millions of lines of legacy code can look at each day as a challenge and a chance to make things better.
